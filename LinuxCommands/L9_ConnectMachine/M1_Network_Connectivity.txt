1) How do i test network connectivity to your Aurora PostgreSQL (Server) ?

    Using (netcat) command.


nc -zv syf-2026....com 5432


nc              →   netcat tool (used for network debugging)
-z              →   just scan, don’t send data
-v              →   verbose (shows result)
syf-2026....com →   your Aurora endpoint
5432            →   default PostgreSQL port



Output :

Success 
        Connection to syf-2026....com 5432 port [tcp/postgresql] succeeded!

Failure 
        nc: connect to syf-2026....com port 5432 (tcp) failed: Connection timed out

        Your machine cannot reach Aurora
